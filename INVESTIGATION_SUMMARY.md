## 1. Embedding Model Investigation

### Test Results

**Old Model (text-embedding-3-small)**:
- R1-R5 similarity: **75.5%**
- Status: **Paper-validated model**

**New Model (sentence-transformers/all-mpnet-base-v2)**:
- R1-R5 similarity: **77.6%** (WORSE)
- Rating convergence: **2.98-3.06** (no differentiation)
- Conclusion: **Does not improve performance**

**Reasons**:
1. sentence-transformers performed **worse** (higher similarity = less differentiation)
2. OpenAI model is what the paper used successfully
3. Paper achieved ρ = 90% with text-embedding-3-small
4. Documentation predictions were **simulated**, not actual test results

**Files Changed**:

## 2. Demographic Conditioning Investigation

### Paper Findings (from ultra-deep analysis)

**Critical Discovery**:
- **WITH demographics**: ρ = 90-92% (paper Table 1)
- **WITHOUT demographics**: ρ = 50% (random correlation)
- **Improvement**: +40 percentage points from demographics alone

### Demographic Attributes Used (5 core attributes)

From paper page 4, Section 4.3:

1. **Age** (18-80+ years) - HIGHEST impact on replication
2. **Gender** (Male, Female, Non-binary, Other) - MODERATE impact
3. **Income Level** (6 brackets: <$25k to $150k+) - HIGHEST impact (second to age)
4. **Location** (City, State, Region) - MODERATE impact, product-dependent
5. **Ethnicity** (8 US Census categories) - VARIABLE impact, product-dependent

## 6. Expected Performance (from paper)

### With Demographic Conditioning

**Correlation Metrics**:
- **ρ = 90-92%** - Correlation with human ratings
- **K^xy > 0.85** - KS similarity (distribution matching)
- **Pearson R = 0.72** (p < 10^-9)

**Rating Characteristics**:
- **Rating spread**: > 2.0 (strong differentiation across 1-5 scale)
- **Distribution**: Concentrated on 4-5 (realistic purchase intent)
- **Variance**: ~0.1-0.2 across surveys

**Demographic Effects** (replicated from human behavior):
- Age: Concave pattern (middle-aged rate higher)
- Income: Low income → lower purchase intent
- Gender: Less consistent replication
- Location/Ethnicity: Product-dependent

### Without Demographic Conditioning (Control)

**Correlation Metrics**:
- **ρ = 50%** - Random correlation (no predictive power)
- **K^xy = 0.91** - High distribution similarity (paradoxically)
- **Pearson R = 0.14** (not significant)

**Rating Characteristics**:
- **Rating spread**: < 0.5 (convergence to mean)
- **Distribution**: Uniform or biased toward 4-5
- **Variance**: Very low (no differentiation)
